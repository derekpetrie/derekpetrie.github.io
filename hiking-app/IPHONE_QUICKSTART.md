# iPhone Quick Start Guide

Get the Wind River Hiking App running on your iPhone in 5 minutes!

## Step 1: Deploy to Vercel (2 minutes)

1. Go to [vercel.com/new](https://vercel.com/new)
2. Click "Continue with GitHub"
3. Find and select: `derekpetrie/derekpetrie.github.io`
4. In "Configure Project" settings:
   - **Root Directory**: Change to `hiking-app/`
   - Keep everything else default
5. Click "Deploy"
6. Wait for the green "Visit" button to appear (~1 minute)
7. Click "Visit" to see your live app!

**Your app is now live!** Example URL: `https://wind-river-xyz.vercel.app`

## Step 2: Install on iPhone Home Screen (2 minutes)

1. On your iPhone, open **Safari** (important - must be Safari, not Chrome)
2. Go to your app URL (the one from step 1)
3. Wait for the app to load
4. Tap the **Share** button (box with arrow at bottom)
5. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
6. Enter a name: `Wind River` (or your preferred name)
7. Tap **"Add"** in top-right
8. App now appears on your home screen! 🎉

## Step 3: Use the App (1 minute)

1. Tap the **Wind River** app on your home screen
2. App opens in full-screen mode (no Safari UI)
3. Explore trails, view maps, read reviews, download GPX files
4. Works offline thanks to our service worker!

## Troubleshooting

### "Add to Home Screen" button doesn't appear?
- Make sure you're using Safari (not Chrome)
- iOS 13 or newer required
- The button may appear after a second page load
- Or try waiting 2-3 seconds after page loads

### Maps not showing?
- Check your internet connection
- Clear Safari cache: Settings > Safari > Clear History
- Close and reopen the app

### Updates not showing?
- Close the app completely (swipe up in app switcher)
- Reopen the app
- Or force refresh: Pull down on page and release

## Advanced Options

### Update Custom Domain (Optional)
If you have a custom domain, go to Vercel dashboard:
1. Project Settings > Domains
2. Add your domain
3. Follow DNS setup instructions

### Share with Friends
- Share the URL with anyone
- They can also add it to their home screen
- No app store needed!

### Automatic Updates
- Every time you update the code and push to GitHub
- Vercel automatically redeploys
- Next time you open the app, it has the latest version

## Tips for Best Experience

1. **Keep It Updated**: Check back occasionally for new trails and features

3. **Add to Favorites**: Favorite your most-hiked trails
4. **Share Reviews**: Help other hikers by sharing your experiences
5. **Bookmark Trails**: Use iPhone's built-in bookmarks to save favorites
